Required Form Fields

Your Google Form must include the following fields (exact names):

  • company_name - Company Name (must match exactly)
  • year - Year (e.g., 2024)
  • quarter - Quarter (1, 2, 3, or 4)
  • revenue - Quarterly Revenue
  • arr - Annual Recurring Revenue
  • arr_projection - End-of-Year ARR Projection
  • headcount - Total Headcount
  • gross_profit - Gross Profit
  • gross_profit_percent - Gross Profit %
  • sales_marketing - Sales & Marketing Expenses
  • research_development - R&D Expenses
  • general_administrative - G&A Expenses
  • eop_cash - End of Period Cash
  • eop_runway - Runway in Months
